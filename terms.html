2 Service Description & Radar Scope

BrandLeads operates an autonomous B2B intelligence platform designed specifically for commercial web design agencies, digital marketing consultants, SEO professionals, and software service providers. The Platform scans publicly accessible municipal business corridors, evaluates commercial profiles across public search directories and map ecosystems, and identifies digital infrastructure gaps, including:

  • Missing Websites: Commercial entities indexed with positive customer ratings that do not maintain an active, registered HTTP/HTTPS domain URL.
  • Unclaimed Business Profiles: Public local listings marked as unverified or open for business ownership claim.
  • Insecure SSL / Broken Domains: Registered listings directing traffic to expired SSL certificates, mixed-content warnings, 404/500 server errors, or parked registrar pages.
  • Reputation vs. Digital Presence Mismatches: Entities with substantial local consumer demand (high review volume) suffering digital revenue leakage.
Consultative Pitch Generation: BrandLeads synthesizes consultative cold outreach kits (including diagnostic opportunity scores, phone talking points, and email pitches) strictly for commercial B2B sales development purposes.
